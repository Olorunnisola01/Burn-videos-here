# The Human Pattern

## How Emotion, Identity, Memory, and Social Influence Shape the Way People Think and Behave

**Author:** Adele Olorunnisola

**Project:** Amazon-ready nonfiction psychology book

**Copyright year:** 2026

## Author profile

A man helped by God.

## Editorial promise

This book helps intelligent general readers understand human behavior without reducing people to labels. It explores how emotion, perception, memory, identity, development, relationships, groups, habits, and context interact. It does not promise mind-reading, certainty, manipulation, or diagnosis. Its central discipline is curious observation: ask what happened, what the person perceived, what they felt, what they wanted, what they feared, what context shaped the moment, and what alternative explanations remain possible.

## Reader experience

The reader should finish feeling more curious about people, more honest about personal patterns, more careful with psychological labels, and more capable of communicating across difference.

## Safety and accuracy note

This is educational nonfiction, not a substitute for psychological, medical, or mental-health care. Ordinary human tendencies are distinguished from clinical disorders. Examples are fictionalized composites unless explicitly identified as historical or research-based. Claims will be phrased with appropriate uncertainty and supported by credible references.

## Drafting standard

Each chapter will include an opening scene, a central question, accessible psychological explanation, research context, everyday examples, a paradox or tension, practical applications, reflection questions, takeaways, and occasional text-based graphical elements where useful. The book will contain no image illustrations or image placeholders. The manuscript will be assembled only after all chapter files have been drafted and reviewed for repetition, continuity, and citation integrity.
