# Book Design and Formatting Guide

## Visual direction

The book will contain no photographs, illustrations, generated images, image placeholders, or decorative artwork. Its visual identity will come from premium typography and restrained graphical elements built from text, rules, arrows, tables, callout boxes, and simple diagrams.

## Approved graphical elements

- Concept maps made from words, boxes, arrows, and connecting lines
- Comparison tables
- Two-column distinctions
- Numbered psychological frameworks
- Pull quotes set as text
- Shaded definition boxes
- Short process flows
- Cause-and-effect chains
- Chapter takeaway panels
- Reflection question cards
- Simple scales, loops, and matrices created with text characters or publishing-tool shapes

## Layout principles

- Use a clear hierarchy from Part title to chapter title to section heading.
- Keep paragraphs short to medium length.
- Use tables only when they clarify a distinction.
- Keep graphical elements monochrome or restrained in one accent color.
- Avoid excessive ornaments, emojis, icons, or visual noise.
- Do not insert fake page numbers; final pagination depends on trim size and typesetting.
- Preserve accessibility by ensuring every diagram can be understood from its surrounding text.

## Suggested publishing format

Prepare the final manuscript for conversion to Kindle, paperback, and hardcover. Use Markdown as the drafting source, then convert through a publishing workflow that supports consistent heading styles, lists, tables, callouts, and page breaks.
